[2026-09-04 Red and white on the horizon Westerheversand Lighthouse in Westerhever, Schleswig-Holstein, Germany(© bluejayphoto/Getty Images)](https://www.bing.com/th?id=OHR.Westerheversand_EN-CA7107631944_UHD.jpg)
Rising above the salt marshes along Germany's North Sea coast, the Westerheversand Lighthouse is instantly recognisable thanks to its distinctive red-and-white stripes. Built between 1906 and 1908, the lighthouse has stood as an icon on the Eiderstedt Peninsula for more than a century, guiding ships through the waters of the North Sea. Today, it remains one of northern Germany's best-known coastal landmarks. Visitors who climb its 157 steps are rewarded with sweeping views of the surrounding scenery.
