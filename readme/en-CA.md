[2026-11-27 Ribbons of grey and gold Butte at sunset, Face Canyon, Lake Powell, Utah, USA(© Jeff Foott/Minden)](https://www.bing.com/th?id=OHR.FaceCanyon_EN-CA9192055868_UHD.jpg)
Hidden among the maze-like sandstone cliffs of southern Utah, Face Canyon offers a striking glimpse into the dramatic landscapes that define Lake Powell, one of the largest human-made lakes in North America. Formed after the construction of the Glen Canyon Dam, the lake transformed once-isolated canyons into waterways that can now be explored by boat, kayak or paddleboard. Towering red rock walls rise above deep blue waters, creating scenery that feels both rugged and serene. Whether arriving at sunset as golden light paints the canyon walls or seeking shade from the midday desert heat, visitors quickly discover that the journey through this remarkable landscape is every bit as rewarding as the destination itself.
