Tucked into the rugged coastline of northern Spain, the 16th-century Hermitage of Santa Justa, featured in today's image, overlooks the crashing waves of the Cantabrian Sea. Built partly into the rock and associated with the relics of Saints Justa and Rufina, the small chapel eventually became a pilgrimage site. Its sheltered stone walls create a striking contrast with the powerful waves and rugged cliffs around it.
