From above, Rose Island looks like a brushstroke of green surrounded by every shade of blue. Just five kilometres east of Paradise Island in The Bahamas, this slender cay trades busy roads and city blocks for sandy shores, shallow waters and an inland lagoon. Its peaceful appearance comes with a touch of movie drama. Rose Island was used as a filming location for the 1965 James Bond adventure Thunderball, including the explosive destruction of the Disco Volante.
