[2026-11-25 When snow meets red rock Sunset Point, Bryce Canyon National Park, Utah, USA(© Colin D. Young/Alamy)](https://www.bing.com/th?id=OHR.SunsetPoint_EN-GB6418180336_UHD.jpg)
Snow transforms Bryce Canyon National Park, blanketing the landscape in white while its fiery orange-red hoodoos rise above the snowy terrain. Seen from Sunset Point, one of the park's best-known viewpoints, these fantastical shapes are part of the Claron Formation, a collection of sedimentary rocks deposited around 50 million years ago and later sculpted by weathering and erosion. Because much of the park lies above 2,440 metres, snow can arrive as early as October. In today's image, snow clings to the canyon's ridges and spires while mist drifts across the cliffs, creating an iconic winter scene in the American Southwest.
