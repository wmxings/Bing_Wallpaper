[2026-11-20 Feasting in the fjords Diving humpback whale, Kvaloya, Troms, Northern Norway(© Espen Bergersen/Minden Pictures)](https://www.bing.com/th?id=OHR.Megaptera_EN-GB2786065203_UHD.jpg)
Off the coast of Kvaløya in Troms, northern Norway, humpback whales move through the cold Arctic waters in search of a proper feast. They visit the region in winter, following migrating herring into the fjords. Humpbacks are known for their remarkable feeding techniques, including bubble-net feeding, in which some whales blow rings or walls of bubbles to corral fish before surging upwards with their mouths open to catch their prey. In today's image, a humpback slips beneath the icy waves under a glowing pastel sky, capturing a moment of tranquillity in Norway's wild north.
