[2026-11-20 Feasting in the fjords Diving humpback whale, Kvaløya, Troms, Northern Norway(© Espen Bergersen/Minden Pictures)](https://www.bing.com/th?id=OHR.Megaptera_EN-IN2800635615_UHD.jpg)
Off the coast of Kvaløya in Troms, Northern Norway, humpback whales move through the cold Arctic waters in search of a proper feast. They visit this region during the winter months, following migrating herring into the fjords. Humpbacks are famous for bubble-net feeding, a remarkable hunting technique in which whales blow rings of bubbles to corral fish before lunging upwards, open-mouthed, to catch their prey. In today's image, a humpback slips beneath the icy waves under a glowing pastel sky, capturing a moment of tranquillity in Norway's wild north.
